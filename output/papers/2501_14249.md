## Benchmark Modalities

**Modality Types:** text, image

**Description:** The benchmark features multimodal questions comprising text-only items and items requiring the integration of text with an image reference (representing approximately 14% of the dataset).

## Analytical Tasks

**Task Types:** factual recall, multiple choice qa, open ended qa, translation, logical and quantitative reasoning, cultural reasoning, visual recognition, visual reasoning, calibration and uncertainty

**Detailed Description:** The benchmark evaluates expert-level factual recall, multiple-choice QA, and exact-match short-answer QA across diverse subjects. It involves translation of ancient scripts (e.g., Palmyrene), logical and quantitative reasoning on world-class mathematics problems, cultural reasoning over local customs and historical trivia, visual recognition and reasoning over multimodal reference images, and testing calibration error to assess model uncertainty.

## Summary

This paper introduces Humanity's Last Exam (HLE), a highly challenging multimodal benchmark consisting of 2,500 expert-written questions designed to test the absolute frontier of human knowledge across dozens of disciplines. By filtering out questions that current LLMs can easily answer, the benchmark provides a highly resistant test of expert academic capabilities, bridging mathematics, humanities, and sciences.

## Key Findings

State-of-the-art LLMs perform poorly on HLE, with the highest accuracy model (o3-mini) achieving only 13.4% accuracy. Furthermore, all evaluated models display high RMS calibration error (exceeding 70%), showing that they frequently generate incorrect answers with extremely high confidence.
