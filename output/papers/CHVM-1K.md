## Summary

The paper introduces CHVM-1K, a benchmark dataset of 1,000 instances designed to investigate value misalignment in LLM-generated texts within the cultural heritage sector. It provides a framework for analyzing how generative models represent sensitive historical, archaeological, and cultural narratives, and whether they align with accepted ethical and cultural standards.

## Key Findings

Identifies systematic biases, misrepresentations, and value misalignments in LLM-generated texts concerning cultural heritage, noting that generative LLMs frequently exhibit regional biases or Western-centric perspectives when describing non-Western cultural heritage.
