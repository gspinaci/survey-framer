## Benchmark Modalities

**Modality Types:** text

**Description:** SuperGPQA is a text-only multiple-choice question benchmark; a multimodal exclusion step was explicitly run during the quality inspection phase to remove visual dependencies.

## Analytical Tasks

**Task Types:** factual recall, multiple choice qa, logical and quantitative reasoning

**Detailed Description:** Evaluates models on advanced factual recall of domain-specific graduate knowledge, multiple-choice QA (featuring up to 10 choices to minimize guessing), and logical and quantitative reasoning (over 42% of items involve calculation or formal reasoning).

## Summary

SuperGPQA is a highly challenging, comprehensive evaluation benchmark designed to test the upper limits of LLM capabilities across 285 graduate-level disciplines using 26,529 multiple-choice questions. Developed via a human-LLM collaborative system and stringent filtering, it establishes a new evaluation frontier where current SOTA models still show significant room for improvement.

## Key Findings

Reasoning-focused models like DeepSeek-R1 and OpenAI's o1 lead performance, scoring around 60-61%, indicating a substantial gap remains in master-to-graduate-level knowledge. Instruction-tuned models significantly outperformed base models. Humanities disciplines like History and Law demonstrated the highest discrimination power, effectively identifying model capability variance due to their demand for context-dependent reasoning.
