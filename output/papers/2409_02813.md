## Benchmark Modalities

**Modality Types:** text, image

**Description:** Combines text-based questions and multiple-choice options with images (diagrams, art, maps, charts). It also introduces a vision-only setting where the entire question and its choices are embedded inside a single screenshot or photo.

## Analytical Tasks

**Task Types:** multiple choice qa, factual recall, visual recognition, visual reasoning, logical and quantitative reasoning, causal and temporal reasoning, interpretation, transcription and correction

**Detailed Description:** Evaluates academic-grade multiple-choice QA involving factual recall, logical and quantitative calculations, visual recognition of objects and features, visual reasoning over complex diagrams, causal/temporal analysis of historical events, aesthetic interpretation of art and music notation, thematic interpretation of literature, and explicit measurement of OCR-based text extraction from images.

## Summary

MMMU-Pro is an advanced and more robust iteration of the Massive Multi-discipline Multimodal Understanding (MMMU) benchmark. It addresses potential statistical shortcuts by filtering out questions solvable by text-only models, expanding multiple-choice options from four to ten, and introducing a vision-only setting where text is embedded directly within images.

## Key Findings

Models experience significant performance drops on MMMU-Pro compared to the original MMMU, with decreases ranging from 16.8% to 26.9%. GPT-4o leads but falls to 54.0% in the standard setting and 49.7% in the vision-only setting, showing a massive gap to human expert performance (~85%). Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting benefits highly structured reasoning subjects but provides minimal or negative impact in interpretive domains like Art and Design. Furthermore, high OCR accuracy does not automatically guarantee strong downstream multimodal reasoning.
