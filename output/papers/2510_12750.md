## Benchmark Modalities

**Modality Types:** text, image

**Description:** Multimodal VQA combining visual artwork images with natural language multiple-choice questions.

## Analytical Tasks

**Task Types:** multiple choice qa, visual recognition, visual reasoning, logical and quantitative reasoning, causal and temporal reasoning, interpretation

**Detailed Description:** Multiple-choice VQA designed to probe seven dimensions of visual art understanding: Instance Identity and Attribute (visual recognition), Spatial Relation, Instance Location, and Instance Interaction (visual reasoning), Instance Counting (quantitative reasoning), and Visual-Inspired Reasoning (causal inference, common-sense, and interpretation of symbolic and narrative meanings).

## Summary

VQArt-Bench is a semantically rich visual question answering benchmark of 14,463 questions designed to evaluate the visual literacy and interpretive reasoning of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) in the cultural heritage domain. Created via a multi-agent pipeline to move beyond template-based metadata extraction, the dataset evaluates deep compositional understanding and symbolic visual interpretation.

## Key Findings

Closed-source models lead, with Gemini 2.5 achieving the highest overall accuracy of 71%, followed by Kimi-VL (67%) as the top open-source baseline. Surprisingly, all models struggle significantly with basic spatial enumeration (Instance Counting accuracy averages under 55% for most open-source models) but perform relatively better on higher-level Visual-Inspired Reasoning, indicating that fine-grained visual localization in complex artistic compositions remains a major weakness.

## Reviewer Notes

- Both analysts largely agreed across all fields. Ciop included 'cultural_reasoning' in analytical_tasks, but following the adjudication rule to prefer a defensible shorter list, this was dropped in favor of 'interpretation' and 'causal_and_temporal_reasoning', which more tightly map to the benchmark's explicit definition of its 'Visual-Inspired Reasoning' dimension (inferring causal intent, symbolic meaning, and narratives).
- Disagreement on gold_beyond_stimulus: Ciop selected 'yes' based on the general complexity of art interpretation, but the paper explicitly states that the pipeline's Judge agent ensures questions are 'unambiguously answerable from the image' and avoids questions answerable only via metadata. Resolved to 'no' following Cip.

**Facets Explanation:** The benchmark's questions and multiple-choice options are synthetically generated using a sequential multi-agent LLM pipeline based on Wikipedia art descriptions. The generation pipeline is explicitly designed to ensure that questions are unambiguous and answerable directly from the visual content of the image, avoiding reliance on external metadata. The evaluation uses a standard multiple-choice format with a single correct answer, and the paper does not address annotator divergence or community-specific readings.
