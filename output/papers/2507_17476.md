## Benchmark Modalities

**Modality Types:** text

**Description:** The benchmark consists of text-based prompts and short answers in French, Spanish, and Chinese, along with English translations for a subset of the tasks.

## Analytical Tasks

**Task Types:** open ended qa, logical and quantitative reasoning, causal and temporal reasoning, cultural reasoning, interpretation

**Detailed Description:** The benchmark evaluates language-specific linguistic reasoning over grammar and syntax; interpretation of wordplay and homophones; cultural reasoning over regional traditions, including multi-step temporal reasoning regarding holidays; and mathematical reasoning utilizing culturally grounded units, currencies, and counting systems.

## Summary

MultiNRC is a multilingual reasoning benchmark of 1,055 questions natively authored in French, Spanish, and Chinese to evaluate LLMs on linguistic, cultural, and culturally grounded mathematical reasoning. The dataset includes manual English translations for the cultural and mathematical items to analyze the reasoning performance gap between native languages and English.

## Key Findings

State-of-the-art models struggle significantly on native multilingual reasoning, with the top model achieving only 49.0% accuracy. While models perform roughly 10% better on math reasoning when questions are translated into English—due to superior cultural context retrieval—cultural tradition reasoning shows no such performance boost, indicating a persistent bottleneck in specific cultural knowledge regardless of language.
