## Benchmark Modalities

**Modality Types:** text, image

**Description:** Combines written context sentences and descriptive text captions with static generated images representing literal and figurative scenes (Subtask A) as well as sequential multi-panel images depicting narrative progression over time (Subtask B).

## Analytical Tasks

**Task Types:** interpretation, retrieval and ranking, sequence prediction, classification

**Detailed Description:** Tasks include ranking candidate images by alignment to literal or idiomatic contexts (retrieval_and_ranking), classifying sentences and their senses as literal versus figurative (classification), predicting the next narrative frame in a visual sequence from candidate images (sequence_prediction), and assessing overall comprehension of figurative language (interpretation).

## Summary

This paper introduces the AdMIRe benchmark for SemEval-2025 Task 1, designed to test and advance multimodal representations of idiomatic expressions in English and Brazilian Portuguese. The challenge features image ranking (Subtask A) and next-image prediction in narrative sequences (Subtask B) to analyze how well models capture non-compositional semantics and distinguish between literal and figurative language.

## Key Findings

The top-performing system achieved human-level performance (e.g., 0.93 Top 1 Accuracy in English Subtask A), but required intensive ensemble methods, chain-of-thought prompting, and mixture-of-experts pipelines to succeed. The task revealed that generative vision-language models have a strong inherent bias toward literal interpretations of idioms, necessitating advanced mitigation strategies by participants.
